## Training engine at a glance

- **Load model:** Banister CTL/ATL/TSB, computed as exponentially weighted moving averages. Each
  week is planned from the athlete's state on its Monday, plus the weeks they actually completed.
- **Zones:** Seiler's three-zone model.
  - Sessions carry targets in the athlete's own watts, bpm or pace when a threshold is known.
  - Otherwise they are by feel (RPE).
- **Intensity:** polarised. Hard sessions make up at most 25% of a week, and there is no tempo.
- **Progression:**
  - Hours and TSS grow at most 10% a week over the last completed load week.
  - There is a recovery week every 3–4 weeks.
  - Before an event: a taper, then the race-week routine.
- **Levels:**
  - The level (beginner to pro) is unlocked automatically from fitness in the primary sport.
  - Weekly ceilings are set per sport.
  - Double days start at the advanced level.
- **Multisport and switching:** every sport has its own ramp. A new sport starts from a share of
  the fitness built in the other sports, so switching never restarts the athlete from zero.
